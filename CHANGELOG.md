# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-07

- **A release is published by hand.** `scripts/release.sh` installs, tests and publishes from a clean checkout of HEAD, then tags and pushes, signed in to npm with Egildo's security key. `.github/workflows/release.yml` is gone, and with it the token and the provenance attestation; the record is *a release moves three remotes at once*, amended 2026-11-07.

- **A clean slate in every specimen.** Each links the same `reset.css` first: margins, paddings, heading sizes and weights, table and cell defaults, sub and sup, links, background printing and the sixteen margin boxes' alignment are stated rather than left to the engine, from a tag-by-tag comparison of WeasyPrint 69's and Chromium's defaults. A cell's width now includes its padding. Each specimen writes its root as `html:root`, because Cicero's `:root { line-height: 1.65 }` was beating a bare `html` rule and setting every specimen but `bolletta` at the wrong leading. Under BelType `trattato` goes from 220 pages to 189 and `paper` from 4 to 3.
- **Fixes the comparison found.** `bolletta`'s invoice table was wider than the page and made Chromium shrink the page to fit; its summary table now has a plain width, because WeasyPrint ignored the `max-width`. `trattato` states its running matter's type on each margin box, because Paged.js handed the font on `@page` to the book's text (123 pages, now 189). `provvedimento-imu` spans its stamp with `1 / 3`, because WeasyPrint does not take `1 / -1`. `bolletta`'s column gap stops using `+`, which Paged.js rewrites at a lower specificity.
- **`bolletta`'s logo is a grid item through a `<div>`, with its colours on its shapes.** As an inline `<svg>` directly in a grid, spanning two rows, it crashed WeasyPrint 69 and 70 (`'BlockContainerBox' object has no attribute 'position_x'`), and its colours from the page's stylesheet did not reach it there. The look is unchanged; the accent is now written on the logo as well as in `tokens.css`.
- **`provvedimento-imu`**, a mail-merge template from a council's IMU assessment, modelled on its JasperReports layout and sample prints with nothing of theirs copied: one notice as a single microdata item fed by five CSV tables and seventeen XForms binds, and `batch.html`, the merged print run of four invented notices in five copies, as the oracle a merge is checked against. Each notice starts on a right-hand page with a first page of its own (named pages, a letterhead background, an envelope window) and numbers its own pages; sections come and go by `:has()` over the data. Liberation Sans and Mono 2.1.5 and Libre Barcode 39 (OFL) come with their sources. `test/provvedimento-imu.test.ts` holds every amount to the cent and every copy to its data.
- **`bolletta`**, a mail-merge template from the studio's electricity bill: microdata fields bound to a JSON-LD data block with XForms binds, a readings table repeated from a CSV described by CSVW metadata, and in `merge/` twelve invented customers with their daily readings, the first of them the studio's own bill to the cent. `test/bolletta.test.ts` holds every bill's arithmetic, and the closure now follows a data block's `csvw:url` and a CSVW file's `url`.
- **`mostra`**, an exhibition leaflet on A4 in Italian about the Roman Empire in the year 117: a porphyry cover framed by gold hairlines, a tabula ansata cut by clip-path, numbered sections in Roman numerals, a drop capital, cards, three coins drawn in CSS, an inline SVG section of a Roman road, a mosaic emblem dissolved into the paper by an SVG luminance mask, and the Empire's map downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (public domain, 408 paths, two blur filters), kept byte for byte with its `SOURCE.txt`, and Liberation Sans 2.1.5 (OFL) from its official release for the family the map's legend names. The exhibition is invented; the map is real. Four pages under Paged.js, checked by eye.
- **`cases/`**, a second kind beside the specimens: reduced test cases, one defect each, with `cases` and `testCase(name)` and an `expects` on each saying what a correct print shows. The test holds them to the same rules as the specimens.
- **The first five cases**, moved byte for byte from a tester's report of 2026-11-03 against the published cli 0.1.3, only each entry renamed to `index.html`: `contatori-a-zero`, `font-senza-nome-postscript`, `url-in-media-print`, `matrice-2d` and `elemento-personalizzato`. Liberation Mono comes with its OFL; the two barcode fonts are the tester's, contributed under this repository's MIT licence.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-30

- **`paper`**, a short scientific article on A4, moved byte for byte from the cli's `samples/paper/` at v0.1.1, with its three woff2 fonts and their OFL licences.
- **`specimens` and `specimen(name)`**: the list, and the lookup, which refuses an unknown name by listing the known ones. Paths resolve from the package's own location, so they hold from source and from `dist/`.
- **`test/specimens.test.ts`** holds the list to the folders and every reference in a specimen to its own folder.
