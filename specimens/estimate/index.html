Estimate for printing a book

Thank you for asking us to print The Salt Roads. The book has 320 pages of text in black on 90 g uncoated paper, 156 × 234 mm, sewn in signatures of sixteen pages and bound in a four-colour soft cover with flaps. The estimate below is for a run of 1 000 copies, delivered to your warehouse in one consignment.

Every amount is the quantity times its unit price, and the totals are the sum of the amounts. Books are taxed at the reduced rate of 4 %.

Terms

We ask for 30 % of the total on acceptance, 30 % when you return the last proofs and the balance on delivery. Proofs are sent within ten working days of receiving the files; the books are delivered within fifteen working days of your approval of the last proof. A further round of proofs, or corrections after the last one, will be charged at the price above.
